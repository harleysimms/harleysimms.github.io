WHAT I DO

Technology is most valuable when it solves a real problem.

My work sits at the intersection of education, technology, operations, and data.

Throughout my career, I've found myself drawn to complicated processes: systems that require too many manual steps, information trapped in disconnected platforms, and technology that creates more work instead of less.

My approach is to understand the problem first, then determine whether better processes, automation, system integration, training, or purpose-built technology can solve it.
